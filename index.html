About Us

Crafting Premium Packaging Since 2010

HY Packaging is a leading manufacturer of custom paper packaging based in Shanghai, China. With over 15 years of experience, we specialize in designing and producing high-quality gift boxes, rigid boxes, folding cartons, and paper cards for brands worldwide.

We are FSC & ISO9001 certified and a Disney-approved supplier. Our factory is equipped with advanced Heidelberg presses, automatic die-cutting, laminating, and gluing lines to deliver exceptional quality at competitive prices.
